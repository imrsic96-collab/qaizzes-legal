QAIzzes Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: August 23, 2026

You can request deletion of your QAIzzes account and associated personal data by sending an email to qaizzes.support@gmail.com.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to qaizzes.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line QAIzzes Account Deletion Request.
  3. Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your QAIzzes account.

What will be deleted

Where applicable, account deletion will result in deletion or anonymization of personal information associated with your QAIzzes account, including account-related gameplay, statistics, and other personal account data.

Information that may be retained

Certain information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other purposes permitted or required by applicable law.

Processing

We will process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable law.
