Privacy Policy for QAIzzes

Last updated: August 23, 2026

QAIzzes ("QAIzzes", "we", "us", or "our") is a quiz application developed and operated by Ivan Mršić.

This Privacy Policy explains how QAIzzes collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the QAIzzes mobile application and website.

Questions: qaizzes.support@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account and authentication information. When you create or access a QAIzzes account, including through Google Sign-In, we may receive and store your name, email address, profile picture if provided by your authentication provider, and authentication identifiers associated with your account.

Quiz and gameplay information. We may store quiz categories and settings, quiz questions and generated quiz content, answers and results, scores and experience/progression, statistics and performance information, multiplayer room information, multiplayer results and rankings, and Premium subscription status.

Technical information. We and our service providers may process technical information necessary to operate and secure the application, such as device and application information, IP address, browser or operating system information, diagnostic and error information, and network and connection information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide quizzes and AI-generated quiz content
  • Save quiz results and statistics
  • Provide multiplayer functionality
  • Maintain leaderboards and rankings
  • Provide Premium features
  • Process and verify subscriptions
  • Restore subscription access
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Diagnose technical problems and errors
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. AI-Generated Quiz Content

QAIzzes uses third-party AI services to generate quiz content. Information necessary to generate a quiz may be transmitted to an AI service, including selected category, difficulty, language, number of questions, and other quiz-generation parameters. Users should not enter sensitive personal information into quiz-generation prompts.

4. Google Sign-In

When you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide QAIzzes with information necessary to authenticate your account, such as your name, email address, profile image, and authentication identifiers, subject to Google's privacy policies and your Google account settings.

5. Payments and Subscriptions

QAIzzes offers optional Premium features through a monthly subscription. On the web, payments may be processed through Stripe. On Android, Premium subscriptions may be purchased through Google Play Billing.

Payment providers process payment information according to their own policies and terms. QAIzzes does not directly receive or store your full credit card number when payments are processed by these providers.

QAIzzes may receive information necessary to determine whether a Premium subscription is active, including subscription identifiers, purchase information, and entitlement status.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database, backend services, and application infrastructure
  • Google — Google Sign-In and Google Play services/Billing
  • Stripe — web payment and subscription processing
  • AI service providers — generation of quiz content
  • Other infrastructure or service providers reasonably necessary to operate and secure the application

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or make information available to service providers when necessary to provide application functionality, authenticate accounts, process payments, verify subscriptions, generate quiz content, host and store application data, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, or comply with legal obligations.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own unrelated advertising purposes.

8. Data Storage and Security

QAIzzes uses appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the application and our services is protected using industry-standard encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS where supported.

9. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide QAIzzes and its features. If you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information and account data, subject to information we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your QAIzzes account and associated personal data using our Account and Data Deletion page or by contacting imrsic96@gmail.com.

Please include the email address associated with your QAIzzes account so that we can identify the account and process the request.

11. Children's Privacy

QAIzzes is not directed specifically toward children under the applicable minimum age for independent use of online services in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, object to or restrict certain processing, and request a copy of certain information. Contact imrsic96@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
